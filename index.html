1. Paris, France:-

Paris is one of the most popular tourist destinations in the world. It is famous for the Eiffel Tower, beautiful architecture, museums, art, fashion, and delicious French cuisine. Visitors can also explore famous places such as the Louvre Museum, Arc de Triomphe, and Notre-Dame Cathedral. Paris is often known as the “City of Light.”
Dubai is a modern and luxurious city famous for its impressive skyscrapers, shopping malls, beaches, and entertainment. The Burj Khalifa, the world's tallest building, is one of its most famous attractions. Visitors can also enjoy the Dubai Mall, Palm Jumeirah, Dubai Marina, and desert safaris. Dubai is known for its modern lifestyle and spectacular architecture.
London, United Kingdom

London is the capital city of the United Kingdom and one of the world's most popular tourist destinations. It is famous for its historical buildings, museums, royal attractions, shopping areas, and beautiful parks. Popular attractions include Big Ben, Buckingham Palace, the Tower of London, London Eye, and Tower Bridge.

  • 5-day London trip from India may range from (₹1,00,000 to ₹1,80,000) per person

Singapore

Singapore is a modern island country in Southeast Asia known for its clean environment, modern architecture, shopping centers, and exciting tourist attractions. Popular places include Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay, Sentosa Island, Universal Studios Singapore, and Singapore Zoo.

  • 4-day Singapore trip from India may range from (₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000) per person
  • A Singapore trip should be properly planned by arranging flights, accommodation, travel documents, local transportation, travel insurance, and attraction tickets
  • Visitors can explore Gardens by the Bay, enjoy the attractions of Sentosa Island, visit Universal Studios, take photographs at Marina Bay, and experience Singapore's famous night views.
